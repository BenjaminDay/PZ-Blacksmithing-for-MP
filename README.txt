An improvement on the vanilla blacksmithing system for MP servers.
Blacksmithing works great in single player where you can x3 fast forward through the endless hours of processing materials but on multiplayer it can take multiple IRL hours to process a salvage loot run, which consists of you sitting there watching a bar go left to right and clicking every 30 seconds or so.

This mod proposes a fix for that by introducing a few additional recipes and some missing vanilla recipes that the devs seemingly forgot to add.


- 10 new crucible recipes!
Instead of smelting one by one for each type of metal and size, you can now just fill the crucible with the exact amount of that size / type of metal to get a full crucible in one go!

- Forge Iron Pipes using the new forgeable Railroad Spike
- Draw steel wire and electrical wire using the draw plate
- Cast Steel Ingot and Bar moulds, no more clay needed!
- Smash an Iron Ingot into Scrap Metal using a Sledgehammer for recycling tools
- Convert Unusable Sheet Metal Scrap into Scrap Metal
- Make Engine Parts (and the nuts and bolts required)
- Repair Katana (note only non broken katanas are accepted so be careful and you cannot make a katana from scratch either for balance reasons)
- Forge a replacement car hood for your car
- Smash Light Bulbs into Broken Glass
- Dismantle Radio parts into Electronic Scrap

Packing Items now included! 35 new boxed items and a handful of respective packing recipes added for convinience!

If you think there are other missing recipes the devs should have added let me know and I will consider adding them to this mod in the future.
