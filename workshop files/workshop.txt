title=Blacksmithing for MP
description=An improvement on the vanilla blacksmithing system for MP servers.
description=Blacksmithing works great in single player where you can x3 fast forward through the endless hours of processing materials but on multiplayer it can take multiple IRL hours to process a salvage loot run, which consists of you sitting there watching a bar go left to right and clicking every 30 seconds or so.
description=
description=This mod proposes a fix for that by introducing a few additional recipes and some missing vanilla recipes that the devs seemingly forgot to add.
description=
description=
description=- 10 new crucible recipes!
description=Instead of smelting one by one for each type of metal and size, you can now just fill the crucible with the exact amount of that size / type of metal to get a full crucible in one go!
description=
description=- Forge Iron Pipes using the new forgeable Railroad Spike
description=- Draw steel wire and electrical wire using the draw plate
description=- Cast Steel Ingot and Bar moulds, no more clay needed!
description=- Smash an Iron Ingot into Scrap Metal using a Sledgehammer for recycling tools
description=- Convert Unusable Sheet Metal Scrap into Scrap Metal
description=- Make Engine Parts (and the nuts and bolts required)
description=- Repair Katana (note only non broken katanas are accepted so be careful and you cannot make a katana from scratch either for balance reasons)
description=- Forge a replacement car hood for your car
description=- Smash Light Bulbs into Broken Glass
description=- Dismantle Radio parts into Electronic Scrap
description=
description=Packing Items now included! 35 new boxed items and a handful of respective packing recipes added for convinience!
description=
description=If you think there are other missing recipes the devs should have added let me know and I will consider adding them to this mod in the future.
description=
